## Grammar: Gallery / catalogue

Why it won: the visitor's real question is **"what are the options, and what does one look like?"** That is a collection to walk through, not an argument to follow. Thirteen collections plus six worked examples is a catalogue. Why the other seven lost:

- **Filmic one-shot:** carries a burden of proof. A single linear argument doesn't fit thirteen parallel products.
- **Chaptered editorial:** taken by Master Your Career Path, and a long-read doesn't suit a $1 impulse product.
- **Live surface:** there is no software to demonstrate. A fake prompt generator is banned.
- **Continuous world:** no real geography, and the most expensive build.
- **Typographic poster:** the asset here is moving image, not a sentence.
- **Split stage:** no two-sided argument.
- **Rhythmic cutlist:** an energy grammar. The owner chose calm and cinematic.

Rules this build accepts: the argument-shaped pinned type act is out; no single hero claim; **no copy laid over media**; labels carry fact, never persuasion; every object has the **same label schema**; no kinetic headlines, spotlight or magnetic CTA; **at most one scrub on the whole page**.

**Nav, hero, close.** Nav is an **index of objects** (click to jump). The hero is **object one, already in view and already labelled**, with no separate title treatment. The close is **the last object**: the Mega Bundle, typeset exactly like every other label, so the ask reads as part of the collection.

## Standing rules applied

- **Diverse representation.** The six examples already span cultures and settings (Inuit, a San José lab, a family, a workplace). Keep all six, in that spread. Add nothing generated. Cast any future example across a range of nationalities and ethnicities, never one default.

## Taste-floor decisions

- **Type:** two families. A display face with weight for titles and a clean text face for labels, plus tabular figures for the counts. Final choice at build time; not Inter, which the skill discourages as a default.
- **Colour:** six roles, one accent. Ground near-black with a blue tint (never pure black). The pink is the one accent. The teal is reserved for the tally, as a region, not confetti. All text to 4.5:1 on its real background.
- **Depth:** offset, tinted shadows and edge light on the objects, in place of the current glows.
- **Mobile is art-directed separately:** objects stack vertically, each with its film above and its label below. The index becomes a short sheet. Tap targets 44px.
